About EduNova Classes (ICL)

Concept-Based Learning. Practice-Oriented Understanding.

Empowering Learning, One Student at a Time

At EduNova Classes, we believe learning goes beyond textbooks. Our mission is to help students understand each concept deeply, apply it confidently, and grow academically with a strong foundation.

We follow a concept-based learning model that focuses on clarity, practical knowledge, and individual attention. Regular practice, doubt-solving and progress tracking ensure results.

With a team of 120+ highly qualified and passionate teachers, our educators don’t just teach — they inspire confidence, simplify tough topics, and make learning enjoyable.

“Learn the Concept. Master the Subject. Succeed with Confidence.”

Our Vision

To make high-quality, personalized education accessible to every student in Bengaluru through tailored tuition and consistent mentorship.

  • Provide high-quality, concept-based learning that builds strong academic foundations.
